Here is a quick rundown of the updates I just made to the generic Quiz App.

First off, I completely revamped the look and feel. I moved away from the old purple glassmorphism style and adopted a fresh, modern "Peach & Terracotta" theme. This makes the app feel much warmer and cleaner. I also widened the main game card to give it a more spacious, desktop-grade appearance while keeping everything nice and tidy.

Functionally, I made some big improvements for mobile users. The "Start Quiz" button is now perfectly centered with a lively pulse animation, and crucially, it vanishes completely when you start the game so it doesn't clutter your screen. I also fixed the scrolling issues so you can see all the questions without anything getting cut off.

I added a really cool custom confirmation popup for when you want to stop the game, replacing those boring browser alerts with a stylish yes/no dialog. Plus, I styled the "Quit" button to match the rest of the theme properly.

Overall, the code is now much more robust, responsive, and just looks better! Keeps the fun going without the technical glitches.
